Medium spice

Chicken Samosa Bites

£1.99

5 pieces

Five crisp, bite-sized samosas filled with tender chicken, potato and onion, fragrant with curry leaves and warming spices.

How it’s made

The filling is cooked in small batches with garlic, ginger, mustard, fennel, garam masala, Kashmiri chilli, cumin, turmeric and carom seeds. Each samosa is folded by hand and deep-fried fresh for a golden crunch.

Medium spice

Chicken & Cheese Puff Pastries

£1.99

2 pieces

Flaky golden pastry filled with tender chicken, mature cheddar and a Sri Lankan-style potato filling.

How it’s made

Chicken and potato are cooked with onion, garlic, ginger, curry leaves, mustard, fennel and warming spices. The pastry is finished with butter and sesame, then baked or air-fried until crisp.

Medium spice

Fish Bun

£1.99

1 piece

A soft handmade bun filled with flaky jack mackerel, potato, onion and leeks, richly seasoned in a Sri Lankan style.

How it’s made

The filling is cooked with curry leaves, garlic, mustard, fennel and aromatic spices. Enriched yeast dough is shaped by hand around the filling, brushed with butter, topped with sesame and baked or air-fried.

Sweet

Butter Cake

£0.50

1 piece

A soft golden slice with a tender crumb and delicate Madagascan vanilla aroma.

How it’s made

Butter and caster sugar are creamed with care, then combined with free-range eggs, flour, fresh whole milk and vanilla. The cake is baked until lightly golden, cooled, cut and packed by hand.

Sweet & aromatic

Masala Tea

£1.50

1 cup

A comforting Sri Lankan-inspired milk tea with a warm fragrance and smooth, gently sweetened finish.

How it’s made

Yorkshire black tea, whole milk, cardamom, cinnamon, cloves and ginger are brought to the boil together so the spice aroma develops naturally. Served freshly made.

Contains caffeine

Herbal Coffee

£1.25

1 cup

A fragrant Sri Lankan-style black coffee made from our gently sweetened house blend of coffee and warming spices.

How it’s made

Coffee beans, coriander, cumin, dried ginger, cardamom, cinnamon, cloves and black pepper are ground into a fragrant blend, then boiled with water and sugar for each order.
